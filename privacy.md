# Privacy policy: Ismahan AIOS

Ismahan AIOS is a personal tool used only by its owner. It reads her own Gmail, Google Drive and Google Calendar data, read-only, on her own computer, and AI assistants she chooses (such as Claude) process it to help her organise her information. The data is not shared with anyone else, sold, or used for advertising. Access can be removed any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions.
